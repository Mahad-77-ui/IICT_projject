Care & Repair

Preserving the brilliance, structure, and sentimental value of every Zaverat creation.

Daily Preservation

Step-by-Step Care Routine

Four mindful habits designed to keep precious metals luminous and delicate stone settings secure through years of wear.

01

Wear Last

Put jewellery on after skincare, perfumes, and hair products have dried.

02

Clean Gently

Wipe with a soft lint-free cloth or mild soap and lukewarm water where appropriate.

03

Store Separately

Keep pieces in individual soft pouches or lined compartments to prevent scratches.

04

Inspect Periodically

Check clasps, jump rings, and stone settings every few months.
