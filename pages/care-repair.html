Daily Care Recommendations

Preserving Your 18K Gold Vermeil:

Vermeil is a thick 2.5 micron 18k gold coat over 925 silver. Keep it away from saltwater, chlorine pools, and perfume mist. Clean only with a dry flannel or microfiber polishing cloth. Never use ultrasonic baths.

Caring for 925 Sterling Silver:

Silver naturally reacts with sulfur in the air over time. The best prevention is wearing it frequently, as skin oils maintain its luster! To remove tarnish, use gentle warm soapy water and a soft toothbrush.

Caring for Organic Pearls:

"Last on, first off." Put pearls on after makeup and perfumes have completely dried. Wipe down after each wear with a soft damp cloth to remove perspiration. Store flat so silk cord does not stretch.

Caring for Hand-Forged Brass:

Brass develops a rich, antique patina with age. If you prefer high golden shine, rub gently with lemon juice and a pinch of baking soda, rinse with water, and thoroughly dry.
