Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about orders, shipping, materials, sizing, returns, and caring for your Zaverat pieces.

Ready-to-ship pieces are inspected, polished, and dispatched from our studio within 1–2 business days. Once shipped, delivery takes 2–4 business days to major cities including Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi, and Rawalpindi, and 4–6 business days to other regions across Pakistan.

Made-to-order and Custom Builder designs require 7–10 business days of bench crafting before dispatch.

Yes. We provide complimentary insured shipping on all orders over PKR 10,000 anywhere in Pakistan. Orders under PKR 10,000 carry a flat insured courier fee of PKR 350. Every parcel is tamper-sealed, fully insured until signature on delivery, and packaged in a protective anti-tarnish Zaverat pouch and rigid gift box.

As soon as your parcel leaves our bench, you will receive an email and SMS with your courier tracking number, or you can check your status anytime on our Order Tracking page.

Need to update a delivery address or gift note? Reach out via Contact Us within 12 hours of placing your order so we can adjust it before dispatch.

Our sterling silver pieces are crafted from hallmarked 925 silver (92.5% pure silver alloyed for lasting strength). Our 18k gold vermeil uses the same solid 925 sterling silver base, finished with a thick 2.5-micron layer of genuine 18k gold—far more durable than standard flash plating.

Select heritage cuff and kundan collections also use reclaimed recycled brass sealed with a protective finish; each product listing specifies its exact metal composition.

Yes. We partner directly with vetted lapidary workshops in Northern Pakistan and trusted regional cutters to source natural onyx, turquoise, garnet, moonstone, and cultured freshwater pearls. Every stone is hand-selected in small batches for colour harmony and ethical provenance, and each bezel or prong setting is inspected under magnification before leaving the bench.

All Zaverat 925 sterling silver and 18k gold vermeil designs are 100% nickel-free, lead-free, and cadmium-free, making them comfortable for daily wear even on sensitive skin. Earring posts and hooks are crafted exclusively in solid sterling silver or gold vermeil.

For rings, wrap a non-stretchy strip of paper snugly around the base of your finger, mark the overlap in millimetres, or measure the inside diameter of a well-fitting ring. For bangles, measure across the widest part of your closed knuckles. Visit our Size Guide for full PK/US conversion tables and necklace drop visuals.

Yes. Most solid 925 sterling silver and 18k gold vermeil bands can be resized up or down by up to 2 sizes at our studio bench within 4–6 business days. Fees typically range from PKR 1,800 to PKR 3,800 depending on stone settings and re-plating needs. Eternity bands and full-filigree rings cannot be resized and are noted on their product pages.

Through our Custom Builder, you can choose your metal finish, gemstone cut, chain length, and optional engraving. Once confirmed, our artisans handcraft your piece in small batches over 7–10 business days. For bridal sets or heirloom recreations, we share a sketch and PKR quote within 48 hours of consultation.

Unworn, unaltered pieces from our standard collection may be returned or exchanged within 14 days of delivery, provided they remain in original condition with all tags, anti-tarnish pouches, and gift boxes intact. Refunds are processed to your original payment method or as instant store credit within 5 business days of studio inspection.

Custom Builder creations, engraved pieces, bespoke bridal orders, and specially resized items are handcrafted specifically for you and are non-returnable and non-refundable. However, if a custom piece arrives damaged in transit or differs from your approved specifications, we will repair or remake it at no cost to you.

Every Zaverat creation carries a 12-month craftsmanship warranty covering manufacturing defects, loose stone settings, clasp failures, and solder joints under normal wear. Within your first year, you also receive complimentary prong inspections and studio polishing. Normal wear-and-tear scratches, chemical damage, or lost stones from impact are repaired at modest workshop rates.

Always put jewellery on last—after perfumes, lotions, sunscreen, and hairsprays have fully dried. Remove your pieces before showering, swimming, or exercising, and store each item separately in the Zaverat anti-tarnish pouch away from bathroom humidity and direct sunlight.

Gently wipe your piece after wearing with a dry, lint-free polishing cloth. Solid 925 sterling silver can occasionally be rinsed in lukewarm water with a drop of mild soap and patted dry immediately. Avoid soaking or using ultrasonic cleaners on 18k gold vermeil, pearls, turquoise, or foil-backed kundan settings.

Submit an assessment request through our Care & Repair page with your jewellery type and notes. We will confirm an upfront PKR estimate within one business day and arrange either an in-person workshop drop-off or insured return courier across Pakistan.
