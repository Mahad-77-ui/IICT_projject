Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions.

🔍
How can I place an order?

You can browse our collection, add your favourite pieces and complete checkout. We offer Cash on Delivery across Pakistan as well as secure direct bank transfer.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept Cash on Delivery (COD) in all Pakistani cities, Direct Bank Transfer (IBFT via Meezan Bank), and Visa/MasterCard debit and credit cards.

How long does delivery take?

Standard courier delivery takes 3 to 4 business days. Priority Express delivery reaches major cities within 24 to 48 hours.

What are the delivery charges?

Nationwide standard delivery is Rs. 250. Orders above Rs. 3,000 qualify for completely Free Shipping across Pakistan!

Are the jewellery pieces hypoallergenic?

Yes! All our pieces are 100% lead-free and nickel-free, forged from solid 925 sterling silver or 18k gold vermeil, safe for sensitive skin.

How should I store and care for my handmade jewellery?

Always store your jewellery in the provided airtight linen pouch when not wearing it. Avoid direct perfume sprays, and polish gently using a soft microfiber cloth.
